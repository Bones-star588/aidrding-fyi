Research interests

My current questions sit where visual perception, language, and uncertainty meet.

  1. Vision-language models

    How multimodal models align visual evidence with language, and where that alignment can fail.

  2. Selective prediction

    Methods for recognizing when a model should answer, defer, or abstain.

  3. Trustworthy AI

    Reliability, interpretability, and appropriate uncertainty in deployed systems.

  4. Autonomous driving scene understanding

    Perception and reasoning in complex, safety-critical visual environments.
